## 🛠️ What I build

- **Scheduling layers on Jobber** – van territories, round planning on 4- and 8-weekly cycles, daily capacity targets, missed-visit handling
- **AI voice agents** – answer every call 24/7, call new leads back within 60 seconds, book straight into the CRM (Retell AI + Twilio)
- **CRM integrations & migrations** – two-way sync, webhooks, and data moved between CRMs through their APIs
- **Lead intake** – every channel (forms, Facebook, canvassers, partners) landing in the CRM as real quotes

Jobber stays the record: everything I build writes back to it, and the business owns the software, with no per-seat fees.
